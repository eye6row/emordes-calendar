Itinerary / Draft

Day 1 · Fri May 14

Arrive Nice

Land NCE. Train or bus to Cannes (~30–40 min). Walk La Croisette, first look at the Palais des Festivals red carpet.

Day 2 · Sat May 15

Red carpet + beach

Red carpet crowd spotting. Night: Cinéma de la Plage, free beach screenings at Plage Macé.

Day 3 · Sun May 16

Screenings

Public screenings via Cannes Cinéphiles (free, apply ahead). Try Quinzaine, Semaine de la Critique or ACID sidebar public tickets.

Day 4 · Mon May 17

Day trip

Antibes, Nice old town, or ferry to Île Sainte-Marguerite.

Day 5 · Tue May 18

Dress-up day

Red carpet look. Black tie required for evening Palais screenings. Rooftop or beach club evening.

Day 6 · Wed May 19

Fly home

NCE → SFO.
