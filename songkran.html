Itinerary / Draft

Apr 10–12 · Chiang Mai

Land, settle inside/near the old city. Wat Phra Singh. Saturday Wualai market (Apr 10), Sunday Walking Street (Apr 11).

Apr 13–15 · Chiang Mai

Songkran

Old city moat water fight zone, all day. Waterproof pouch, quick-dry clothes, water gun.

Recover

Maybe an ethical elephant sanctuary (~$70–100). Night markets.

Apr 16 · CNX → BKK

Fly to Bangkok

Domestic flight (~$40–80). Check in near Silom.

Apr 16–22 · Bangkok

Silom nights

Silom Soi 4: Stranger Bar, Balcony, drag shows. LesHi Bar, Goddess Bar. Leftover Songkran water fights on Silom Road.

Apr 23 · BKK → SFO

Fly home

Round trip estimate $700–1,200.
